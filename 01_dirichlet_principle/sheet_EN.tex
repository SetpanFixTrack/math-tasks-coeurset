\begin{enumerate}
    \item A box contains 10 black socks and 10 white socks. What is the minimum number of socks you need to pull out without looking to guarantee that you have at least one pair of socks of the same color?
    
    \item There are 30 students in a class. Prove that there are at least two students whose last names start with the same letter.
    
    \item Prove that among any $n+1$ integers, one can choose two whose difference is divisible by $n$.
\end{enumerate}
